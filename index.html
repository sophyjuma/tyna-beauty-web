About Us

Tyena Beauty Training College was established in Machakos, Kenya, by Christine Gatwiri, a qualified beauty professional with extensive experience in beauty training. The institution began by providing informal training to students seeking practical beauty skills and has grown organically through community demand and word-of-mouth referrals. The College is now formalizing its operations under the TVET Authority to provide nationally recognized certifications


We are located at Machakos Town, Machakos County in the Eastern Region of Kenya. Tyena Beauty Training College is registered as a business in Kenya and is in the process of full accreditation under the TVET authority as required by the Technical and Vocational Education and Training Act (2013). We have embraced Kenya’s Vision 2030 ambition of focusing on the transformation of the education system in Kenya. We are focused on being one of the best Technical and Vocational training institutions in Kenya.
